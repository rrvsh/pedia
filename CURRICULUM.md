# Personal Curriculum

This document will outline my personal curriculum for 2026. The curriculum is constructed using a model of "curriculum as praxis" - this means that the act of learning and improvement is the goal in and of itself.

It shall be separated into topics, that will include both theory, practice, and assessment.

It should be reviewed along with ASSIGNMENTS.md every Monday morning.
