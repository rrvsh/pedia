# Personal Curriculum

This document will outline my personal curriculum for 2026. The curriculum is constructed using a model of "curriculum as praxis" - this means that the act of learning and improvement is the goal in and of itself.

It shall be separated into topics, that will include both theory, practice, and assessment.

## Topics

### Primary Subjects

- End-to-end web development
    - Infrastructure and configuration as code (declaration/automation)
    - Web interface design (accessibility/aesthetic)
    - Data models and flow (input/persistence/output)
- Worldbuilding
    - Speculative history
    - Geography and ecology
    - Economy and trade
    - Culture, language, and religion
    - Knowledge development and transmission (tech/magic)
    - Governance, law, and geopolitics
- Narrative writing
    - Entertainment and turn of phrase
    - How to provoke thought
    - Clarity and conciseness
    - Knowing what to write about that is interesting

### Secondary Subjects

    - Speedcubing

- Physical development
    - Staying in shape
    - Eating well

- Media consumption
    - Games
    - TV Shows
    - Movies
    - Fiction

## Daily Schedule (provisional)

### 0500 to 0600: Morning routine

Do all the following in order:
- Brush teeth first thing in the morning
- Eat the frog (hardest thing to do that day)
- Morning pages (freewriting in journal at least one full page)
- Shower
- Plan out first sprint

### 0600 to 0900: Encyclopedia development

_Optional on weekends. Alternative: do puzzles._

Objective: produce a writing artifact.

Pick one of:
- Active research
- Work on writing an encyclopedia article
- Work on writing a narrative piece (cannot be directly from one source)

### 0900 to 1800: Software engineering (day job, personal projects)

_Optional on weekends. Alternative: freeform media consumption._

As long as the focus is always on building a software artifact, work can be freeform.

Note: must take one exercise break and one lunch break.

### 1800 to 2100: Last sprint

_Optional on weekends. Alternative: freeform media consumption._

- Pick a book, movie, TV show, or game.
- Set a goal to reach (# of chapters, episodes, etc.)
- Take process notes and review once done
- Review should include synthesis into my personal encyclopedia

### 2100 to 2200: Night routine

Do all the following in order:
- Take melatonin
- Make a frog (choose the thing you most don't want to do)
- Place filled water bottle and pre-pasted toothbrush by bed
- Check alarms are set
