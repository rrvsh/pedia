# Daily Playbook

## Morning routine

Do all the following in order:
- Wake up to alarm at 5am
    - Put toothbrush in mouth before turning off alarm
    - Turn on lamps
    - Go to toilet
    - Drink water
- Make coffee
    - Roll and smoke cig
- Morning pages (freewriting in journal at least one full page)
    - Also top priority
- Eat the frog (hardest thing to do that day)
    - By any means necessary - this has priority over the rest of the day
- Shower

## 2100 to 2200: Night routine

Do all the following in order:
- Take melatonin
- Prepare whiteboard:
    - Frog -> the thing you most don't want to do.
- Prepare the desk:
    - Monitor shows `playbooks/daily.md`.
    - Ashtray and all smoking supplies in the drawer.
    - Journal on desk with pen, date and daily goal (if any) pre-written.
    - Keyboard charging.
    - Rubbish thrown.
    - Corners empty.
- Prepare side table:
    - Pre-pasted toothbrush.
    - Filled water bottle.
    - Phone charged with alarms set.
        - Place out of reach (important!)
        - If using phone in bed, MUST place out of reach.

## Monday to Saturday

In order:
- morning routine
    + Get ready for first task (be very serious about this)
        - Clean up desk (throw rubbish and ash)
        - Hide smoking supplies
        - Finish coffee and put in sink
        - Fill water bottle
        - Choose music
        - Get distractions away
+ Work on daily goal
    - Do all the following in order:
        - Get OBS ready to record
        - Start task for 5 minutes
        - Start recording, full screen camera
        - Plan out task for two hour window, half screen camera
        - Execute, producing artifact, quarter screen camera
        - End recording
        - Make coffee
+ Software engineering
    - Take caffeine at start of block. Pick one software engineering task (NOT configuration) and begin working on it. Break is only allowed at 12pm - from 12pm to 6pm there are no expectations.
    - If you start on a task during the day, stop and think if it could be tomorrow's daily goal.
    - Make sure to perform the daily review by 8pm and transcribe into the monthly note.
- Nightly routine:
    + Prepare whiteboard:
        - Daily goal -> something concrete and presentable.
- Start on tomorrow's goal for 5 minutes.

## Sunday

In order:
- morning routine
- weekly review (extremely top priority)
- nightly routine

### Weekly review process

- collate inbox:
    - all unreviewed process notes
    - all unreviewed daily notes
    - all video logs
- process into:
    - archived notes in ~/0_library/{notes,video_logs}/archived/*
    - daily summaries in monthly note
    - weekly summary in yearly note
    - ~/1_repos/pedia entry
        - incl. ASSIGNMENTS.md
    - ~/1_repos/site_content article
    - 1 edited youtube video with thought through concept, title, description, and thumbnail
